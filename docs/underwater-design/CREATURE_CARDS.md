Developer-only proposal.24 forms, not implemented. Each native sprite is16×16; portraits32×32. Written shape distinctions are art acceptance criteria, not inspected pixels. All damage figures are base Q4 before existing phase/combat rules. Existing five-phase cycles remain unchanged.

**Control:** Face the range bracket before R; no tracking after startup.

Startup12 updates; active18; lifetime≤36; cooldown90; target damage≤16Q4. At most1 moving entities, six enemy slots with spawn-generation receipts.

**Versus existing commands39,52:** Range-band occupancy, not a marked weapon follow-up or close square rush arrest.

**Field utility:** echo_outline. Exact tagged targets and actual cast proof only.

**Acquisition:** The keeper introduces the cuttlefish after the two echo-plaque observations.

**Second individual:** After a retained Scriptcuttle or Fanfolio, return to the keeper and compare a different pair of large torn-outline leaves. A second Inkbud chooses to join after an explicit invitation.

**Branches:** 50 or 51; both are tier2, each needs its own same-individual trial. This base command is inherited by both branches.
