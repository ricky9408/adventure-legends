# Underwater chapter: Nacreway and the Palinode Archive

Developer design proposal only. Nothing here enables or implements a form, room, command, item or save revision. Do not turn this document, its trial solutions or acquisition list into player-facing screenshots or promotional media.

## The chapter in one paragraph

Nacreway is a inhabited underwater town with a market, listening benches, a shellwright, a sanctuary and residents who disagree warmly about an old procession. A surviving map suggests that both remembered routes once existed. The player follows its missing coastline into the Palinode Archive and discovers how two accounts fit together. The goal is to recover a shared story and make room for both versions. This is an exploration-and-discovery chapter, not another broken-utility repair tour. The world is bright pearl, coral, jade and warm brass with deep blue-green shadows, readable faces, moving fronds, and clean silhouettes.

## A town worth returning to

Nacreway's keeper offers two observations, not an exposition dump. The shellwright demonstrates a reversible buoyancy shelf. A cook, a frame-maker and a returning dancer each own a different side of the procession story. Their optional quest changes the public mural. The sanctuary supports the existing party, equipment and explicit branch-evolution interfaces. A listening bench is a calm reset point, with visible interaction range and no ambush. Six optional sidegrade items include a sword, lance, body piece, boots, belt and ring.

Every building is an original fictional place. Shell vaults, kelp screens, ceramic picture frames and bronze joints are materials and visual motifs, not claims about any specific real culture. No Nintendo characters, music, maps, ripped sprites or extraction tools are used.

## Main-route guarantee

Entry follows Magma's main quest32. It does not require the Core ending or an optional old quest. Quest38 grants Inkbud49 after an observation lesson. Quest39 grants Bobclam52 after a manual, ability-free return loop. These two unevolved bases plus the protected starter sword1 are sufficient for all mandatory puzzles and the finale. Their signatures are learned at level1; new recruits arrive at party-median level clamped26–32, with bond20. Main progression never requires an evolution, an optional companion, a drop, a purchased tool, a grinding threshold or a branch trial.

Inkbud teaches echo outlines. A visible dotted trace exposes tagged contours, sound shadows and already-drawn hidden mechanisms; it does not reveal arbitrary secrets across the map. Bobclam teaches ballast shifts on tagged shelves. A shift is a reversible two-state arrangement, not a free vertical swimming system. The player still has direct, responsive planar movement with the existing collision footprint. Underwater atmosphere must not add inertia, oxygen attrition, movement slowdown or mandatory breath equipment.

The vestibule uses a positive/negative map overlay. The stacks use shelf footprints and a far-side return landing. The listening room uses occlusion and an intentional empty space. The finale uses observation, a reversible environment change, readable attack openings and the starter sword. This deliberately avoids repeating one three-switch-and-carrier puzzle across eight rooms.

## Companions and branches

Exactly24 reserved forms49–72 are proposed, covering immutable familiesF017–F024. Every family remains base → branchA OR branchB. Each terminal is tier2, never a disguised tier3. All16 target paths have separately authored same-individual trials. Eight deterministic repeat invitations provide real second bases. A repeated interaction cannot duplicate the same invitation, and a new invitation cannot clone a previous individual's trial, experience, bond or identity.

The cast includes cuttlefish, chalk clams, leafy seahorses, horseshoe creatures, warm tube-worms, soft sea stars, articulated eels and comb-jellies. Water6, Wood6, Metal6, Earth3 and Fire3 forms are spread across visibly different materials, locomotion and action shapes. Both Yin and Yang remain an independent axis, with explicit per-form polarity where a branch changes it. Wind, light, heat and sound are effects, not extra phases. No polarity means good or evil.

All24 command cards have a measured shape, time, control rule, target budget and specific comparison with existing commands. Examples include a wall-tangent rim crawl, a perforated stamp, a manually drawn warm trail, a folding two-hinge ribbon and alternating triangles with a permanently empty diagonal. Evolution keeps the base command available and preserves field utility. Branches offer different jobs, not just a higher damage number.

## Puzzles, discoveries and fairness

Every puzzle has a clearly drawn RESET pedestal reachable outside moving geometry. Reset costs no health, currency, item or earned receipt. Wrong configurations give visible feedback and can be reversed. Death, load or leaving a room clears unfinished trial proof, not previously earned progress. No hidden pixel hunt is accepted: interaction targets are at least16×16, important puzzle targets at least20×20, ordinary passages at least24 pixels wide.

Hints come in three layers: an environmental motif, a short resident/sign clue, then an optional explicit explanation on repeated inspection. Shapes, outlines and movement communicate every rule without relying only on color or sound. Completed key discoveries remain visible or have a clear journal/map marker; repeated sources use a visibly distinct invitation state.
