# Five phases and polarity: scope of this implementation

Traditional 五行 names Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water. The generating cycle is Wood → Fire → Earth → Metal → Water → Wood; the controlling cycle is Wood → Earth → Water → Fire → Metal → Wood. “Five phases” emphasizes relationships and change. [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Wuxing](https://iep.utm.edu/wuxing/)

Yin and yang form a complementary dimension, not two additional members of that five-phase set. Historical interpretations are varied; this game chooses equally valuable complementary roles, not a good/evil or gender hierarchy. [Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Yinyang](https://iep.utm.edu/yinyang/), [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: Han Dynasty philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/han-dynasty/)

Every creature assignment, numerical multiplier, evolution condition and ability is original game design inspired by those ideas. Wind remains an ability, not a sixth phase. No historical, religious or scientific authority is claimed for the game's invented rules.

The current native catalog implements phase/polarity lookup and tests all 25 phase-pair reference multipliers. The legacy campaign's damage is deliberately unchanged; those battle modifiers are not enabled yet. Field capabilities remain explicit and inherited independently of phase, so a Water label alone never grants diving or opens a gate. Broader phase combat and Water/Metal acquisition are later milestones.
