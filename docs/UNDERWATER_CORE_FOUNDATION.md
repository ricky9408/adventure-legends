## Bounded admission job

Timing exposed a whole-roster admission scan that cannot fit a frame at large
collections. The new private singleton cursor owns164 bytes on ARM and validates
every record and all duplicate identities in calls bounded to four records.
No caller-supplied trusted-valid flag, heap, fullSave copy or roster copy is used.

The caller owns an exclusive immutable snapshot throughout the operation, and
separately proves fullSave validity plus typed source/quest/attempt authority.
The core independently checks party, history, every occupied/empty record,
identity uniqueness, story receipts and prospective72-opportunity coverage.
Begin returns a fresh generation token; status is unavailable before completion.
Commit compares all4140 live-roster bytes exactly with the snapshot, consumes the
token once and executes the small mutation. Declines, stale state, replacement,
cancel, malformed records and denied admissions preserve every live byte.
Snapshot and live must be distinct objects. Cancellation is required on load,
death, scene changes, source/attempt invalidation and abandoned confirmation.

Public API: `creatures_admission_job_begin`, `step`, `result`, `commit_grant`,
`commit_evolution`, `cancel`. The synchronous legacy APIs remain intact.
