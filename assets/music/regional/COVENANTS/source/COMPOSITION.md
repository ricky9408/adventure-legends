# The Roads That Stay

Rooms 70–77: shared work, patient ferry and Last Watch. Twenty 4/4 bars at 100 bpm = 48 seconds. An eight-bar long-breathed statement is followed by a compact four-bar E-minor bridge and eight-bar return/settling coda. The opening held third B is followed by the small A–G connector and an upward fifth to D: a patient question, distinct from the G–D opening of the earlier road tune. Warm C-major sevenths and first-inversion G soften the working pulse. The bridge reaches G5 once, then B7's D-sharp gives a genuine E-minor arrival at bar 13. This is a reharmonized return, not an arbitrary key shift. The coda walks down C–G/B–Am7–D7, gathering people under a descending bass line before the loop resolves into G. Bass remains even half notes; inner voice sings through gaps and longer upper tones. Last Watch's tension is carried by restraint and release rather than new percussion or a hostile dungeon figure.
