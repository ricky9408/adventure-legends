# Room for Another Traveler

The form deliberately uses an eight-bar conversation, a six-bar widening middle, and eight-bar return/coda; the duration is achieved with a complete phrase, never a mid-bar cut. The gently dotted A–F-sharp–E–F-sharp invitation is unrelated to HOME's D–F-sharp–E–A opening. The last four bars become a communal coda rather than simply copying the first four. Bass is entirely regular half-note support. The melody occupies C-sharp4–D5, leaving the upper register clear, with phrase-end breathing spaces; inner is pianissimo.
